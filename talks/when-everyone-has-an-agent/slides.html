04

Better decisions

Preferences become vectors

Buyers are points: me, and everyone like me.

Products are points in the same space.

Recommending means finding what’s near.

Nearness is relevance r.
Price tips the choice from C to A.

06

Recommendation as a game

Competing recommenders pick locations

Recommender 2

Each recommender agent chooses which point to put in front of my agent.

My agent takes the best offer.

That is a
facility-location game.

Ben-Porat & Tennenholtz, NeurIPS 2018 · Ben-Porat, Rosenberg & Tennenholtz, AAAI 2019 · Tennenholtz & Kurland, CACM 2019

07

Why the game matters

Competition changes what gets shown

One recommender

Maximizes its fee: shows C, D, E

Buyer picks C

Buyer utility

190

Platform fee

$39.50

Two competing recommenders

Both show A

Buyer picks A

Buyer utility

320

Platform fee

$15 each

Equilibrium: neither recommender gains by changing its list, given the other’s. Every pure equilibrium shows A, and the buyer gains 130.

Assumes the buyer buys only displayed items, compares lists at no cost, and prices are fixed; the commission is split equally.
Cf. Inderst & Ottaviani, AER 2012 · Walunj et al., Performance Evaluation 2025

09

When the players are LLMs

What changes when agents play the game

Strategies are language

Pitches, explanations, and framing become the actions.

Rationality is bounded

Agents approximate best responses; they don’t compute them.

Behavior depends on the model

Swap the model and the market outcome moves.

Adaptation in context

Agents learn within a conversation, not only across rounds.

Each one strains the assumptions behind the equilibria we just saw.

Dütting, Mirrokni, Paes Leme, Xu & Zuo, WWW 2024 · Dubey, Feng, Kidambi, Mehta & Wang, KDD 2024

10

Evidence

Agents take the first offer

60–100%

of the time, LLM buyer agents chose the first proposal they received.

Third proposals: near zero. A 10–30× first-mover advantage across every model tested.

Keeping markets open

Subsidize exploration

My agent’s attention is scarce. It will stick with known merchants.

The market agent pays a bonus for reviewing a new or unfamiliar merchant.

Discovery stays open, and incumbents can’t lock in.

Kremer, Mansour & Perry, JPE 2014 · Mansour, Slivkins, Syrgkanis & Wu, OR 2022 · Wang et al., RecSys 2023
