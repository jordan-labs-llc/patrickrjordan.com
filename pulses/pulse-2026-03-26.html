Weekly Insights

  • 🔐 UCP Identity Linking gets mechanism registry pattern — future-proofing authentication
    PR #265 transforms identity linking from hardcoded OAuth 2.0 to an extensible mechanism registry. Businesses declare supported_mechanisms arrays; platforms select via TLS-style negotiation.
